Gulp It — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026 · Applies to the Android app Gulp It (package com.codmmontage.gulpit), developed and published by codmmontage (“we”, “us”).

The short version
  • Gulp It does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account of ours, and we run no servers of our own. Your progress lives on your device.
  • The game uses three Google services: AdMob for optional rewarded ads, Google Play Billing for in-app purchases and Google Play Games Services for optional leaderboards. Google processes data for those services under its own privacy policy.
  • We have no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of our own, and we never sell data.

1. Data stored on your device

Gulp It saves your game progress (your pond, animals, fireflies, hats and decorations) and your settings (sound, music, options) in the app’s private storage on your device. If you drop your own art into the game’s custom-art folder, it stays there too. The game also keeps a small local record of milestones you have reached — that you hatched, molted, or finished the tutorial, and when — which helps us improve the game if you ever choose to send it to us with a bug report. It contains no identifiers of any kind and is never uploaded automatically. None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No registration, login, name, e-mail address, phone number or password with us.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files outside the app’s own folder.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs other than the Google services described below. The milestone record in section 1 stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

3. Third-party services in the game

The following services are built into Gulp It. They are operated by Google LLC and handle data according to the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive from them what is needed to run the feature, as listed here.

ServiceWhen it is usedWhat it may processWhy
Google AdMob (rewarded ads) Only when you tap to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads never play on their own. Your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, app package name, language and coarse region, and how you interact with the ad (viewed, closed, clicked). To select, show and count ads and to confirm that you earned the reward. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can choose non-personalised ads.
Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) When you buy a firefly bundle or the “no ads” option. Google Play handles the payment. The app receives the product ID and a purchase token so it can deliver what you bought. We never see your card, bank or billing details. To deliver and, if needed, restore your purchases. Purchase history is kept by Google under your Google account; you can review it at play.google.com.
Google Play Games Services (leaderboards and cloud backup) Only if you choose to sign in from the Rankings tab. Nothing happens if you skip it. Your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar, player ID) and the scores the game submits (your current totals for the leaderboards). Google shows these on the leaderboards according to your Play Games privacy settings. If you are signed in, the game also stores a copy of your saved game in your own Google account using Play Games Saved Games, so you can recover your pond on a new device. That copy holds only game progress — the same data described in section 1 — and it goes to your Google account, never to us. To let you compare your pond with friends, and to keep your progress if you change or reset your phone. Restoring a backup is always something you choose from Settings. You control what other players see, and can remove your Play Games data and saved games, from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.
Google Play (the store) Automatically, as with any app installed from Google Play. Google may collect aggregated install, crash and “app not responding” statistics and share them with us in anonymised form (Android vitals). To help us keep the game stable. This data does not identify you to us.

4. Permissions

Gulp It itself requests these Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state — used solely for the Google services above.
  • Vibration — for the short buzz on in-game events. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Notifications — for optional reminders that something is ready at your pond. You are asked before any are sent, and you can decline or turn them off later.

The Google advertising and billing libraries add further permissions of their own when the app is built. These are declared by Google’s code rather than written by us, and they are listed on the app’s Play Store page:

  • An advertising ID permission and Google’s Ad Services permissions, used by AdMob only when you choose to watch an ad.
  • A billing permission, used by Google Play Billing when you make a purchase.
  • Basic phone state, wake lock and foreground service permissions, which the advertising library declares as part of its standard installation.

Gulp It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files outside its own folder.

5. Children

Gulp It is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through one of the Google services above, please contact us and we will help you get it removed, or use Google’s own tools for the relevant account.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Game progress and settings: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Android’s app settings.
  • Ads: in Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID and to opt out of ad personalisation. If you gave consent in the EEA/UK, you can change it from the game’s settings screen.
  • Leaderboards: sign out of Google Play Games, adjust your Play Games profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data for Gulp It from the Play Games app.
  • Purchases: managed through your Google Play account.
  • Anything else: e-mail us (below) and we will answer within 30 days.

7. Security and retention

Because the game keeps your progress only on your device and we run no servers, there is no personal data held by us to secure or retain. Data processed by Google is kept and protected under Google’s policies and your Google account settings.

8. Legal basis (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies: personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; delivering purchases and leaderboard scores you asked for relies on performance of a contract; anonymised stability statistics rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working. You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game gains a feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. The current version is always at the address you are reading now.
